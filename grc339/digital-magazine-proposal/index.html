Vision: a Chicano focused fashion magazine

VISION MAGAZINE

Vision magazine is a Chicano fashion magazine that celebrates and highlights identity, culture, and self expression through style. The magazine would include iconic looks, modern staples and fashion designers from the Chicano community. Through the use of bold typography, impactful photography, and cultural iconography included in the layouts, Vision celebrates heritage while displaying modern interpretations of Chicano fashion.
